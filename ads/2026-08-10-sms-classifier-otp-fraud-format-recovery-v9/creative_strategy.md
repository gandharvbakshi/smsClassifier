# Creative Strategy

## Decision

The current ads communicate `OTP warning`, `review before sharing`, and `account change`, but they do not consistently say the familiar user problem—OTP fraud. This challenger names that problem in the first read while preserving the product's differentiating proof: the action an OTP appears to authorize.

## Controlled experiment

- Control: the existing live mechanism-led/account-change assets.
- Challenger: explicit `OTP fraud` language plus the same `For Account change` product state.
- Fixed variables: campaign, budget, bidding, location, selective-optimization events, app, proof state, pricing, and incumbent assets.
- New assets: one concept rendered natively at 1:1, 1.91:1, 4:5, and one 9:16 deterministic product-proof video.

## Claim controls

Use `help protect`, `check`, and `may authorize`. Never say the app stops, blocks, or detects every fraud attempt. The app reads the SMS; it does not listen to a caller.

## Measurement

Primary: cost per distinct paid-attributed `default_sms_set`. Secondary: `first_sms_classified`, `trial_started`, and `purchase_verified`. Guardrails: app removals, CPI, and delivery concentration. Do not declare a winner before seven days, two days of conversion lag, and at least 20 total qualified conversions.
