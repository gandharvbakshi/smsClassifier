# Product Context

SMS Classifier is an Android SMS app that helps people understand what an OTP appears to authorize before they share or use it.

## Primary User

An everyday Android user in India receiving an OTP while a caller, chat contact, or service interaction creates urgency. A secondary buyer or advocate is an adult helping parents or family members avoid OTP social-engineering scams.

## Core Job

When a code arrives, show whether it appears to be for an account change, card or bank transaction, UPI or PIN action, login, KYC or e-sign, app action, or delivery confirmation. This lets the user compare the displayed purpose with the action they intended.

## Reason To Exist

Generic organizers help users find OTPs. Broad fraud tools warn about suspicious senders or conversations. SMS Classifier's strongest reason to exist is action-level OTP purpose at the before-sharing decision moment; the experiment now names the OTP-fraud problem explicitly while keeping this mechanism as proof.

## Supporting Value

- Focused OTP inbox and one-tap copy
- Message organization as a default SMS app
- Broad scam warnings and do-not-share guidance
- A 14-day Pro trial for cloud scam checks and risk reasons, then ₹199/year

## Claim Boundary

The app analyzes SMS content. It does not listen to calls, know what a caller said, guarantee that a message is fraudulent, or prevent every scam. Safe wording is: the app shows what the OTP appears to authorize, helping the user notice when it does not match the action they expected.

## Proof Boundary

Use deterministic synthetic message data. Product UI may show an account-change purpose and do-not-share guidance. The caller's claimed purpose belongs in the ad scenario or headline, not inside product UI as if the app heard it.
